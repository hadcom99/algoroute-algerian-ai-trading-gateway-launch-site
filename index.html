How it works

Live in production in under 90 minutes

Four steps from sign-up to routed, DZD-billed AI requests.

1

Top up your DZD wallet

Visit your AlgoRoute dashboard, select CIB, Edahabia, or Baridimob, and add credit in Algerian dinars in under 90 seconds. A compliant DZD invoice is generated automatically and stored in your account for accounting exports.

2

Drop in one API key

Replace every existing provider key in your codebase with your single AlgoRoute key — it is fully compatible with the OpenAI SDK, so no request schema changes are required. All models across LLM, image, voice, and video providers are immediately accessible.

3

AI Trader routes each request

For every inbound call, the AI Trader engine scores available models against live cost, latency, and quality vectors in under 12ms, then dispatches to the optimal provider. You pay the routed rate — not the default rate — from the first request.

4

Monitor spend and performance

The real-time dashboard shows per-request routing decisions, DZD cost breakdowns by model, and latency percentiles across your entire request history. Set spend guards to cap daily or monthly usage before a runaway process hits your wallet.
